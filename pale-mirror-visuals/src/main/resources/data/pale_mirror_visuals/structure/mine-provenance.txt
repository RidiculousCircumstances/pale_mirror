Pale Mirror private mountain-mine blueprint provenance (schema v40)

Runtime structures live in the pale_mirror_visuals namespace and are compiled through the PM sanitizing
loader. The loader ignores structure entities and block-entity NBT, removes jigsaws, spawners, traps and
loot containers, and replaces freely mineable ore/raw-material states with inert rock.

Active frontier-industrial composition
  surface: portal_hoist, crew_outpost, processing_hall, power_house, loading_yard and maintenance workshop
  underground: entrance_adit, iron_gallery and controller_chamber
  staged Red Valley: dispatch_foundation, dispatch_shell, dispatch_machinery and dispatch_commissioning
  PM additions: typed functions/slots, bounded local foundations, circulation, safety details and climate transforms
