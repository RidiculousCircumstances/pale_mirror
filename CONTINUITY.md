## Key decisions
- The world plays itself: settlement simulation creates production, demand, trade, rationing, investment, migration, recovery and decline; Narrator selects legible stories but never creates or freezes those processes. PM-caused physical effects have no ownership-, parcel- or operation-defined safe zone: their real Minecraft consequences are durably observed and reconciled into canonical damage, casualty, terrain/infection or bounded physical-scar facts. Every resident and bioform remains one physical actor (never a cohort); canonical resource stock has a versioned 4096-point fixed scale and registered warehouse containers expose ordinary real Minecraft stacks (ORE: 64 ingots = one canonical unit), with player supply/theft/destruction entering atomic receipts. The normal living-world profile is layered: physical actors tick at 20 TPS, tactical decisions update at sub-second cadence, and one indivisible canonical source day takes 24,000 game ticks (20 minutes); FAST_GRAYBOX retains 1,200 ticks/day plus explicit complete-day fast-forward.
- 0.4 introduces FOOD and an agrarian settlement alongside the existing IRON economy. Local infection remains a stressor; global territorial infection economy is a later milestone, followed by organized military settlements/expeditions.
- Economic resolution uses deterministic snapshot -> intentions -> market/route clearing -> simultaneous commit -> consequences/events. Iteration order must not change outcomes.
- Inter-settlement money uses one mutual-credit accounting unit: every purchase creates equal buyer debt and seller credit, the network net balance remains zero, and bounded credit limits prevent unlimited imports.
- Currency does not replace physical logistics, safety reserves or the resolver. Prices choose among feasible trades; route capacity, stock, production and survival policy remain authoritative constraints.
- Player economic identity is a StoryAudience account. Earning credits creates a settlement obligation and spending transfers that claim; initial negative player credit, exact price formation and credit reputation remain unapproved design details.
- Region, economy, facilities, routes, population and physical incident outcome are global. Knowledge, access and scenario response are private per StoryAudience; physical help is shared and the first canonical outcome closes linked scenarios once.
- Authored fresh-world geometry comes from deterministic immutable chunk-local plans. Post-gen change uses persisted jobs, never force-loads prospective chunks and never overwrites unknown player changes by default.
- `TOWNSHIP` is the only materialized settlement stage in 0.3. The 12/24/72 compositions and reserved expansion parcels are validated plans, not active growth transitions.
- Settlement/mine structures use curated NBT with semantic entrances, bounded terrain integration, connected streets/adits, typed parcels and Foundry fail-closed validation; no generic-box fallback or global terrace is allowed.
- Baseline rail is a persisted dry-preferring route graph with loaded-chunk materialization, bridge limits and topology-based health; Red Valley/Create capability remains blocked until physically validated.
- Population movement is a persisted off-screen `WorldJourney`; nearby residents materialize under stable identity leases. Shelter selection prefers the player choice and falls back only after the fair grace window.
- Harvester creature authoring is image-faithful: the pinned base-reference pixels are the sole likeness authority and the canonical editable model is its direct organic-mesh reconstruction, with no Minecraft-style simplification. A generated reference/model contour overlay plus independent visual review is mandatory; automation never assigns likeness. Cube-only GeckoLib output is legacy compatibility geometry, never visual acceptance authority.
- The literal Collector image remains the locked source-local 0° anchor and primary-view contour authority. The reviewed v05 source-local cycle (tail end-on 90°, opposite broadside 180°, head end-on 270°) is the declared hidden-side constraint used by the completed shape-only trial. Its normalized six-panel contact sheet is audit evidence, not Hunyuan input; two extra elevated/low panels are Blender-only. Any conflict resolves in favour of the locked 0° trace.
- Harvester reconstruction has a mandatory primary-image trace gate: the locked source-image plane plus versioned source-pixel contour/anatomical traces constrain the editable mesh and decide acceptance, but never require profile/cage/primitive reconstruction. Collector begins only from a hidden immutable raw-v05 copy; its working copy may receive direct local retopology, deformation and merge/delete/create edits on the existing surface, with a full backup and review decision between sequential passes.
- Dominant Collector anatomy uses the artist-authoring protocol: diagnose actual renders first, write an intent card, isolate one semantic region, then sculpt/retopologise the connected existing surface. Broad scripted vertex masks and pixel-curve field deformations are unsuitable for organic mass reconstruction. The user explicitly authorized the protected Master artist channel: it permits named visible Blender keys, pen paths, drags and DPI-correct left-clicks only inside the exact Collector Master window, with foreground verification plus Screen-DC evidence before/after every action; it never exposes arbitrary desktop, shell, path or runtime access.
