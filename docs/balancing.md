# Balance policy

## Threat hierarchy

1. Vanilla hostile mobs are the normal night-time background.
2. Hostile Tactics makes their groups and positioning matter; it must not inflate
   basic mob health/damage or enable routine Create-base griefing.
3. Born in Chaos is uncommon dangerous fauna, never the default night population.
4. Mowzie's Mobs are rare handcrafted encounters.
5. Cataclysm is a major encounter layer.

Spawn rates, if changed, are measured against vanilla entity counts over the same
night-time sample. No edit is accepted simply because a mob "feels too common".

## Combat

Better Combat + Simply Swords define ordinary player combat. Test weapon reach,
attack timing, shield interactions, enchantments, and boss scripts before changing
values. Early Simply Swords gear must not erase the wooden/stone/iron curve; strong
weapons stay tied to discovery or rare loot where the mod's supported data/config
format allows it. We do not silently overwrite recipes with an unreviewed script.

## Transport

Create infrastructure precedes meaningful air travel. Aeronautics recipes remain
upstream defaults until a documented progression proposal and dedicated-server flight
tests exist. Any future gate must use a durable, inspectable milestone—not elapsed
days.

## Caliber

Caliber is optional and disabled. Its acceptance requires an automated ammunition
chain, mid/late-game gate, Better Combat compatibility test, and Cataclysm/Mowzie
time-to-kill measurement. See `create-caliber-evaluation.md`.
