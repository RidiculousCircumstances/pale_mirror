# Visual acceptance checklist

Inspect overview and eye-level views. Record screenshots for each failure.

## Terrain and circulation

- The reserved disk blends into surrounding terrain without cliffs, trenches,
  floating vegetation, cut tree crowns, or foreign structures.
- Main paths read as continuous streets, sit at ground level, and use slabs or
  stairs for intentional half-step transitions.
- Entrances widen to their doors, remain two blocks clear, and do not collide
  with props, posts, fences, or rails.
- Bridges, retaining walls, foundations, rail beds, and elevated structures
  have believable support.

## Architecture and infrastructure

- Civic, residential, industrial, logistics, and defensive buildings are
  distinguishable by silhouette and placement.
- Roof layers close cleanly; chains and hanging props attach to solid geometry.
- Mine surface works read as an industrial yard with a clear portal, loading
  area, and navigable underground transition.
- Rails join through curves, use the intended rail types, avoid fluids, and
  terminate at legible loading/receiving points.
- Perimeter segments explain their purpose, meet the terrain, and provide
  usable gates rather than repeated narrow doors.

## Detail and atmosphere

- Lamps, fences, planters, pallets, and clutter occupy reserved furniture
  slots rather than paths.
- Repetition is deliberate; no stacked lamps, isolated posts, random slabs, or
  cobwebs appear without narrative purpose.
- Material palette, weathering, lighting, and density support a mining-frontier
  identity and remain coherent across modules.
- Healthy, crisis, damaged, recovery, and prosperous states are visibly
  different where the product promises them.
