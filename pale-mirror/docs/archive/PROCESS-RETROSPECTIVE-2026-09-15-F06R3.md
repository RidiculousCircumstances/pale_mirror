# F0.6R3 process retrospective — 2026-09-15

Status: retained factual retrospective, not a workflow authority. The binding
rules are in `docs/engineering-agent-protocol.md`, revision
`2026-09-15-PM-TL-JUDGMENT-LOOP`.

## What consumed time

The exact aggregate wall time by category is not fully reconstructable and
remains `UNCONFIRMED`; retained reports contain individual run durations. The
high-confidence waste patterns are nevertheless explicit in the F0.6R3 order:

- automation promoted narrower facts than the player promise: one crop receipt,
  a route cursor, endpoint blocks or private hive frames passed while ordinary
  ingress still showed resets, an absent field and `ORGAN NOT CURRENT`;
- early carriers stopped before release, successor COLD progress or the second
  restart, so later segments repeatedly exposed a new boundary;
- at least one wrapper admitted a false green because phase labels and motion
  were not independently discriminated;
- cheap architecture/file-size policy ran after an expensive native carrier,
  forcing a structural source change that invalidated candidate identity;
- human contradictions were sometimes answered first with more automation
  rather than immediate exact artifact/world correlation and one product-shaped
  reproduction;
- the active order and ledger accumulated superseded `rNN` narratives, raising
  context reconstruction cost for both roles;
- earlier PM behavior added method approvals and frequent supervision even
  though Terra was responsible for the technical result.

Not all repeated runs were waste. r58 and r63–r75 were discriminating reds that
found real custody, phase, successor and observation defects. r76 established
the first complete lifecycle; r77 corrected exact evidence identity and the
zero-progress-successor oracle without changing the production JAR. The process
failure was allowing expensive discovery to reach those boundaries late, not
the existence of every diagnostic run.

## Corrective design

The unified protocol now requires:

1. autonomous Terra ownership of implementation, diagnosis, test design and the
   delivered result; PM owns product scope, architecture and conformance only;
2. one closure map covering the complete natural lifecycle and strongest known
   contradiction, with `PROVEN_NARROWER` distinct from `PROVEN`;
3. cheap changed tests plus all applicable static/architecture checks before an
   expensive native carrier;
4. a whole-boundary audit after successive failures move along one causal chain;
5. a product-shaped promotion ladder: technical WIP → exact product candidate →
   frozen release candidate → exact deployed human candidate;
6. one terminal aggregate for the frozen candidate and no confidence-only
   repetition of unchanged evidence;
7. exact runtime correlation for a human incident and one consolidated retest
   request only after a successor human candidate exists;
8. hourly accounting by newly established product fact or eliminated material
   uncertainty, with an impasse review after two non-progressing hours;
9. bounded active context, with immutable run chronology archived at acceptance
   and handoff boundaries rather than replayed in the ledger.

The later judgment-loop correction makes these items decision aids rather than
a diagnostic program. The technical lead chooses their form and order by
expected information gain and product value. In particular, a fixed count of
failures, a mandatory cheapest-first technique, a literal one-run recipe or a
required closure-map artifact must not replace causal engineering judgment.

## Expected effect and limit

These rules should remove the largest avoidable loops: proving the wrong slice,
discovering cheap policy failures late, rerunning unaffected gates, repeatedly
asking the user to test WIP and reconstructing obsolete context. They do not
guarantee defect-free implementation or replace native and human evidence. The
remaining F0.6R3 uncertainty is intentionally one direct M3 observation of the
already deployed exact candidate, not another automated proof campaign.
