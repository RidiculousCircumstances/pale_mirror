# Frontier v3 hive metabolism and morphogenesis contract

Status: accepted target contract for `MAT-007`; implementation and evidence are
pending. It is not active implementation authority until MAT-006 is independently
accepted and a work order activates this contract.

## Player promise

The player encounters one living distributed organism whose nutrients visibly
move through its destructible root, whose exact food or captive matter is
digested over time, and whose exact organs and bioforms physically grow from
conserved biomass. Cutting the route, taking the input, opening a chamber or
damaging a growth site changes that same process. Leaving, returning or
restarting changes only execution and presentation; it never teleports cargo,
replays consumption, completes growth on chunk load or creates replacement
matter, organs or creatures.

## Sole owners and composition

- The common exact resource-lot/allocation/custody ledger solely owns nutrient,
  biomass and digestible-input quantity, provenance, claims and custody.
- The canonical hiveroot graph solely owns stable segments, junctions,
  connectivity, condition and independently available vascular and synaptic
  capacity. Infection cells are substrate, never implicit transport edges.
- One canonical organ-network transfer owns each exact in-flight allocation,
  its retained path, capacity reservation, cursor and terminal receipt.
- The hive physiology/lifecycle owner solely owns exact organs, organisms,
  carcasses, structural/reserve biomass, cocoons and capability activation.
- A registered class-E or class-F Scene behavior may execute bodies, pulses,
  stations and observations. It owns no resource, topology, organ, organism or
  lifecycle truth and cannot select a replacement route, input or recipient.

The two seed nests remain roots of one polity, but never share a magical
inventory. Every remote consumer must hold a local exact input or the completed
receipt of one retained transfer.

## Interruptible hiveroot flow

One transfer retains its identity and ruleset version, exact source and target
STORE slots or consumer reservation, exact quantity/allocation and provenance,
ordered stable segment/junction path, per-edge cursor and partial progress,
reserved vascular capacity, phase, custody epoch and typed terminal outcome.

```text
source allocation confirmed
  -> capacity/path reserved
  -> exact allocation leaves source for IN_TRANSIT custody
  -> bounded directional edge work / retained cursor
  -> target admission and physical endpoint confirmation
  -> exact allocation enters target custody once
  -> completed, blocked, stranded, returned, lost or recovery inspection
```

A bounded pulse may represent many fungible units physically; MAT-007 does not
require one item entity per nutrient unit or permanent stack identity. The
canonical quantity, allocation and custody remain exact. Departure debits the
source claim exactly once and arrival credits the target exactly once; a pulse
is presentation evidence, not another cargo ledger.

COLD may advance only along the retained currently known path and capacity.
HOT exposes directional progress on the same segments and commits its cursor
only from typed observation of the current bounded work unit. A severed or
capacity-starved segment, destroyed endpoint, changed slot, player theft or
foreign obstruction affects that exact transfer. It cannot silently jump an
edge, borrow synaptic capacity, choose a new path or source, or let the target
consume an unarrived allocation. A later reroute is a canonical decision that
retains the same cargo/custody and closes the superseded reservation.

A route may cross a partially loaded envelope. Each retained segment is then
either under one eligible HOT physical custodian or under the COLD continuation
after an exact release; adjacent segments cannot create a second allocation or
permit an endpoint credit to jump over an unresolved cursor. Loading only the
target endpoint never constitutes movement or receipt. A natural leave/return
story must preserve the same allocation and cursor while the HOT/COLD boundary
moves across the path.

## Digestion and irreversible consumption

One digestion lifecycle retains an exact operational `DIGESTER`, semantic
ingress/chamber/output stations, exact input allocation or exact carcass/body,
output reservation, conversion ruleset, stages, confirmed consumed quantity,
loss/yield and typed interruption evidence.

```text
exact input claimed
  -> delivered or exact living captive restrained
  -> chamber enclosed
  -> visible digestion progress
  -> durable irreversible-consumption boundary
  -> exact biomass/nutrient output confirmed
  -> completed, rescued, spoiled, blocked, lost or recovery inspection
```

Before the irreversible boundary, ordinary removal, chamber damage or rescue
returns whatever exact unconsumed custody physically remains and changes that
same lifecycle. A living captive stays the same exact actor through restrained,
enclosed and digesting stages; a successful rescue cannot coexist with its
casualty or biomass output. After confirmed irreversible consumption, the
source cannot be recreated or rescued. Output quantity and configured loss are
conserved from confirmed input, never inferred from a timer or nearby item.

Digestion is not an endpoint chest transform. While naturally loaded, its
meaningful stages use the retained chamber/input and, where applicable, exact
actor or carcass. COLD advances those same stages without inventing a physical
observation and cannot cross an unresolved irreversible boundary.

The irreversible boundary is one confirmation state machine over the exact
captive/input and output reservation. Across crash recovery, exactly one of
these is true: the source actor/input is still recoverable, confirmed
consumption owns the conserved output, or the local boundary is
`UNKNOWN_AFTER_RESTART`. A living/rescuable actor and credited biomass may
never coexist, and neither may both disappear without the typed unknown/loss
owner.

## Organ and bioform morphogenesis

Organ growth and bioform growth are distinct retained lifecycles even when
they consume the same transfer and reuse class-F execution infrastructure.

An organ lifecycle retains one stable future organ identity and accepted kind,
one exact hiveroot attachment and immutable bounded footprint, exact local
biomass allocations, ordered spatial growth units, stage, condition and
capability state. Only confirmed completed geometry plus required vascular and
synaptic connection activates the organ's owned capability.

Capability activation applies to the complete immutable required footprint and
connections as one owned gate. Partially current multi-chunk geometry remains
visibly partial and non-operational; a current label, one loaded attachment or
one finished chunk cannot activate the organ while another required unit is
pending, unknown, foreign or damaged.

```text
site/input admitted -> bud -> bounded tissue frontier -> differentiation
  -> connection inspection -> exact organ activation or typed partial failure
```

Player damage or obstruction changes the current frontier and consumes only
confirmed used biomass. Partial tissue is retained aftermath, not silently
repainted or converted into an operational organ. An unsupported organ kind
cannot be fabricated merely because its enum exists.

A bioform lifecycle retains one exact BROOD process, one exact future organism
identity, accepted chassis/mutations, exact biomass allocations and one exact
cocoon or emergence slot. The reserved identity prevents duplication but is
not an active `ActorLocation`, mature organism or Minecraft body before the
canonical emergence transition.

```text
brood/input admitted -> germinating -> morphing -> maturing
  -> emergence transition -> exact age/stage-appropriate organism
  -> cocoon custody, purposeful assignment or typed loss/recovery
```

While HOT, the same slot and growing body expose meaningful retained stages;
opening or damaging it affects that exact organism. While COLD, those stages
may progress under the same rules. Loading a chunk only projects current state:
it never starts growth, creates an organism, matures it early or reenacts an
off-screen emergence. A mature inactive organism occupies its exact cocoon and
wakes only for current work or threat under the existing mobilisation contract.

Emergence converts the reserved future identity into exactly one canonical
actor, stable Minecraft UUID and live body or inactive actor carrier under the
shared actor-carrier contract. Cocoon state, actor creation and physical
binding recover as one exact-once boundary; load order cannot leave both an
occupied cocoon and emerged body, lose the actor, or select a replacement.

## HOT/COLD, demand and recovery

Observer demand never creates metabolism or growth. HOT and COLD share the
same lifecycle IDs, actors/objects, allocations, stages, cursors, ruleset and
history; only physical execution differs. One active physical custodian excludes
competing COLD writes. Demand loss drains after every physical custody or
irreversible-effect boundary, persists the exact continuation and returns it to
COLD without replay. Returning shows current partial flow, digestion or growth,
not missed animation.

Graceful and crash recovery retain exact inputs/outputs, used and remaining
quantity, topology/capacity reservations, stages, spatial cursors, organ/
organism IDs, slots and confirmed consequences. A physically started transfer,
consumption, emergence or capability activation is `UNKNOWN_AFTER_RESTART`
until its exact naturally loaded postcondition is inspected. Ambiguity isolates
only that transfer or lifecycle boundary; it cannot mint input, roll back
player custody, activate a partial organ or duplicate a body. Completed records
compact to bounded receipts only after every allocation, lease, reservation and
unknown boundary closes.

## MAT-007 M2 evidence boundary

Closure requires composed evidence from the production owners:

- pure and codec coverage for conservation, capacity/path admission, COLD
  progress, exact digestion yield/loss, organ and bioform identity/slot
  admission, stale epochs, duplicate confirmation and obsolete-byte rejection;
- focused class-E/F Scene evidence for ordinary retained progress plus severed
  or capacity-starved flow, source/target theft, Digester/chamber loss, rescue
  before irreversibility, damage/obstruction during organ and bioform growth,
  demand loss and restart;
- the smallest candidate-bound ordinary-player native lifecycle set that
  actually demonstrates directional HOT flow, staged digestion and staged
  organ and bioform morphogenesis with exact identities and terminal semantic
  outcomes. It must include a mixed partially HOT/COLD transfer, bracket living-
  captive irreversibility, reject partial-organ activation and retain one exact
  emerged actor through restart. Compatible meanings may share a run, but
  endpoint frames or one asserted label cannot substitute for any continuous
  lifecycle;
- player-height in-progress frames for flow, digestion, organ growth and
  bioform growth, assessed only as M2 physical-presence evidence; and
- one coherent final critical integration gate after all changed faithful
  lanes pass locally.

Existing exact custody, transfer receipts, hiveroot topology/damage, cocoon,
Scene SDK, recovery and MAT-005 expedition evidence may compose unchanged.
Fixtures may establish initial stores, an admitted input or a due growth task,
but cannot inject the asserted movement, consumption, activation or organism.
No standalone infrastructure, timing-ratio, full native matrix or repeated
confidence run is required.

This target closes `MAT-007` at M2 only. It does not accept M3/unbriefed visual
comprehension, final art, natural-terrain hardening, annual balance, complete
infection-front progression (`MAT-008`), resident symptom presentation
(`MAT-010`), every later adaptation/siege mechanic, deployment, production
cutover or v2 removal.
