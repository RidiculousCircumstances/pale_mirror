# Frontier v3 decision-to-visible-consequence contract

Status: accepted target contract for `MAT-009`; implementation and evidence are
pending. It is not active implementation authority before `MAT-008` acceptance
and an explicit work order.

## Player promise

Settlement and Hivemind decisions are not invisible number changes or labels.
A market shortage, company commitment, quarantine choice or hive doctrine uses
only knowledge available to that side, creates retained exact work or refusal,
changes real actors/resources/places, and leaves a discoverable physical and
delayed consequence. The player may intervene in that chain and observe the
same world react; a board explains current meaning but never substitutes for
the operation it claims.

## Authority and causal chain

Each of the twelve settlements and the one distributed Hivemind retains exactly
one first-class `DecisionAuthority` scope under the existing architecture. It
owns perceived inputs, policy/ruleset identity, reconsideration epoch, selected
objective and bounded decision provenance. It references rather than duplicates
knowledge, population, resource, company, task, operation, tactical or custody
state.

Every accepted decision follows one inspectable chain:

```text
bounded perceived fact and current commitments
  -> versioned policy decision with alternatives/reason
  -> retained objective and exact task/operation or explicit refusal
  -> resources, actors, facilities/fronts and execution lifecycle
  -> physical outcome, loss, obstruction or cancellation
  -> delayed canonical consequence and next reconsideration
```

Scenes, boards, diagnostics, entity brains and movement providers cannot create
purpose or select an alternative. They execute or explain the retained choice
and return typed observations. A stale authority epoch, missing perceived fact,
unavailable exact actor/resource or superseded objective fails closed rather
than silently replanning inside an adapter.

## Required decision classes

The product matrix contains at least the following rows. A row is covered only
when its ordinary, unavailable/refusal and intervention or loss paths reach
their own concrete outcomes.

| Decision class | Required retained action | Required physical consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market demand, surplus or price response | Exact order/contract, payer/payee or allocation, producing or transport task | Named workers/facility and exact stock/cargo change, delivery, shortage or cancellation |
| Company/employment commitment | Exact legal company/employment/work-order owner and labor/resource reservation | The same employees leave competing work, perform the retained work or visibly fail/refuse; no phantom output |
| Settlement shortage/rationing | Exact shortage fact, recipient priority and per-recipient allocation outcome | Changed serving/provisioning, missed meals, stalled work or redirected cargo for the same named residents |
| Quarantine/containment | Exact local perceived hazard, policy epoch, affected residents/facilities/routes and care task | Real movement/admission restriction, treatment/decontamination, supply consequence or policy failure without deleting people |
| Settlement security/development | Exact threat/capacity fact, roster/project/resources and objective | Patrol/defence/construction/evacuation or explicit inability, with casualties, damage, capacity or displaced-person aftermath |
| Hive doctrine (`CONSOLIDATE`, `EXPAND`, `INTERDICT` and later accepted policies) | Exact bounded territorial/sighting knowledge, doctrine epoch, task and resource/command admission | Real flow/growth/scouting/expedition/assault/retreat or visible inability, retaining exact organisms and consequences |

Finance beyond already accepted exact zero-sum transfers remains excluded until
the capital, interest and missed-payment rule is explicitly accepted. A future
decision class enters this matrix only with an owning contract; it cannot be
declared covered by a generic objective label.

## Visibility without a second truth

The primary evidence is the physical process and consequence: who moves or
stops, which facility works, what cargo changes custody, which route closes,
where infection advances, who is rationed, treated, mobilized, displaced or
lost. Object-local boards and explicit inspection cards may summarize state,
cause, risk and available response from the current canonical projection. They
do not own history, continuously announce background events, expose opaque IDs
or invent a successful action.

Success must be at least as legible as failure. A completed delivery, recovered
resident, contained hazard or successful retreat needs a current physical/result
surface rather than only disappearance of a warning. COLD decisions remain real
and may complete off-screen; first visibility shows current actors, stock,
damage, tasks and aftermath instead of replaying the choice for spectacle.

## Player causality and competing outcomes

An ordinary player action enters the owning process as typed evidence. Supplying
or stealing the exact resource, opening or blocking a route, killing an actor,
damaging a facility, treating a resident, breaking containment or disrupting a
hive connection may enable, redirect, degrade, cancel or fail that same retained
choice according to its policy. No scene boundary protects consequences and no
adapter quietly substitutes another actor, cargo, route or target.

A decision records the alternatives it actually considered and why the selected
or refusal outcome followed from its bounded knowledge and commitments. This is
operator/audit provenance, not player-facing exposition. The player-facing
world need only make the local cause, threatened value, available response and
result understandable without revealing hidden enemy facts.

## HOT/COLD, recovery and bounds

HOT/COLD switching changes execution detail, never decision input, objective,
task identity, resource cost or result. Physical observations may immediately
invalidate a premise or change the next canonical reconsideration; COLD cannot
countermand an authoritative active lease using unobserved Minecraft state.
Restart retains authority/policy version, epoch, perceived facts, selected
objective, exact child work, receipts and unresolved consequences. Replayed or
stale decisions cannot duplicate tasks, reservations, notices or outcomes.

Every authority has bounded current commitments, history/provenance and
reconsideration cadence in the persisted ruleset. Indexes expose due work and
affected objects without global scans. Terminal decisions compact only after
their exact tasks, allocations, leases and delayed consequences close.

## MAT-009 evidence boundary

Maintain a checked-in criterion matrix mapping every declared decision class to:

- the canonical perceived input, authority/policy and alternative/refusal;
- the exact objective/task/operation and its actor/resource/facility owners;
- ordinary, unavailable and intervention/loss outcomes;
- the physical/result surface and delayed consequence visible on return;
- deterministic replay/restart and HOT/COLD neutrality evidence; and
- the remaining M3 comprehension question.

Closure requires focused pure/codec tests for authority epochs, bounded
knowledge, alternative/refusal, duplicate suppression and recovery. Integration
evidence reaches the real owning work, terminal or blocked fact and downstream
consequence for every matrix row. The smallest candidate-bound ordinary-player
native set samples every distinct physical consequence family without inventing
a scenario per policy label. Existing accepted verticals compose when their
exact decision provenance and inputs are unchanged. One coherent final critical
gate follows the changed faithful lanes.

Native sampling by presentation family is an economy rule, not evidence
inheritance between decision rows. A row is red when its policy/objective exists
but its exact child work has no owner, cannot obtain its retained actor/resource
custody, never reaches a terminal/blocked fact or produces no promised delayed
consequence. Refusal and unavailable alternatives require the same causal
closure as successful selection.

A diagnostic, board, objective record, endpoint counter or before/after frame
alone is not closure. Fixtures may establish perceived facts and resources but
cannot inject the asserted decision, task outcome or delayed consequence. No
standalone matrix-speed campaign or confidence rerun is required.

This target closes `MAT-009` at continuous causal M2 only. M3 remains a separate
unbriefed-player gate: the player must naturally discover at least the sampled
settlement and hive chains, explain the cause and threatened value, choose or
decline a response, and recognize the delayed result without diagnostics.
Final balance, every future policy, natural-terrain polish, deployment,
production cutover and v2 removal remain separate.
