   refill cancellation through the registered stock-loss owner left an
   ActorMovementContext.ExpeditionReplenishment referencing the now absent

   reversible supply replan. A generic registered claim-owner checkpoint hook
   now lets the exact promise owner retire only its typed obsolete motion,
   before stock/finance/title disposition in the same unpublished transaction.
   It reuses ActorMovementProcess.retireOwned with actual command/event time:
   COLD interval and current canonical HOT body keep their supported position;
   no rewind to departure, teleport or fictitious arrival. The group UAE is
   retained. Existing released-activity consequences cancel the exact progress
   schedule and wake the actor. Physical edit composition, native pre-effect
   previews and journal projections pass the explicit canonical clock. A
   stock-only call without the required movement checkpoint fails explicitly.
2. **Stock/cash were mistaken for accessible local supply.** The travel hold
   ignored usable approaches. Shared ActorStorageApproach.readiness probes
   current retained navigation evidence and distinguishes READY, UNAVAILABLE
   and UNRESOLVED. It starts no route calculation or wait subscription. The
   needy member, rather than another well-fed member, must have potential
   access. Known unusable supply cannot hold the group forever; unknown facts
   and pending route calculations remain waits.
3. **A retained refill had no reversible access-loss exit.** The mission may
   now cancel its still-unprepared exact food pickup through the typed
   ExpeditionReplenishmentCancelled event. Policy uses retained-only route
   evidence/current target clearance; acceptance checks the retained identity
   and absence of a physical pre-effect, never reruns route search in replay.
   This is an authorized withdrawal, not a no-path or physical-effect receipt.
   Shared movement checkpoint, claim release, payment cancellation and refund
   into the same mission budget are atomic. Food, title and treasury balances
   do not change. The normal owner can select another usable service station
   or continue its itinerary when no supply is usable. A prepared physical
   transfer retains its existing recovery protocol and cannot be discarded.
