# Local approach work and shipment economics

Accepted scope: repair the measured redundant local approach scans, avoid routing
impossible internal manifests, separate affordability from efficient dispatch,
and assemble fragmented warehouse stock through existing same-owner hauling.

The shared local approach probe is only a positive shortcut. Targets outside its
three-dimensional envelope require no geometry reads. Its bounded edge allowance
may return a miss; the existing cooperative navigator retains complete routing,
unknown/pending distinction, collision profiles, access policies and exact arrival.
It grants no actor, route, service or inventory authority. No family owns a second
pathfinding budget or terrain graph.

Internal manifest policy checks the real unclaimed cargo upper bound before
requesting pickup/drop connectivity. Detailed routing, exact capacity, source
claims, crew/asset admission and durable transfer remain their existing owners.

ReplenishmentLot distinguishes an affordable purchase quantity from an efficient
useful load. Dispatch uses the latter; matching still bounds goods and invoiced
freight against real available cash. Poor funding alone cannot classify a
one-unit remainder as an economical trip. Explicit uncovered supply deadlines
can still admit the only affordable recovery, including one unit. No universal
minimum, free goods, new credit or artificial demand is introduced.

StorageConsolidationSupply supplies derived placement requests to the existing
ResourceHaulingPolicy. It considers operational same-owner warehouses, respects
filters and priority, and tops up a partial transport stack at the larger preferred
stock location. Actual incoming shipments stabilize its target; a pending receiver
does not become a reverse source. Dining pantries and production/local buffers
keep their own placement policies. Unclaimed source stock, current receiving
capacity and existing flow admission bound each ordinary InternalHaulRequest.
The existing shipment/UAE/navigation/custody/recovery owners execute every move;
there is no multi-container instantaneous pickup or second stock ledger.
