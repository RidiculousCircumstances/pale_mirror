# Frontier v3 resident condition and contact contract

Status: accepted target contract for `MAT-010`; implementation and evidence are
pending. It is not active implementation authority before `MAT-009` acceptance
and an explicit work order.

## Player promise

Hunger, exposure, infection, injury and recovery belong to the same exact
resident the player can meet, feed, protect, endanger or treat. While that
resident is nearby, their body and behaviour derive readable condition cues and
real environmental contact changes the same canonical condition immediately.
Leaving lets bounded autonomous consequences continue; returning shows the
person's current state, not a healthy replacement, replayed symptom or label
disconnected from their body.

## Sole truth and derived presentation

`ActorCondition` remains the sole owner of vitality, nutrition, exposure,
disease/infection and recovery facts for one exact resident. `HumanPopulation`
owns that resident's identity, household, membership and life/death transition.
Provisioning, medical/service work, environment, combat and physical aftermath
submit typed causes to those owners; none keeps a private health counter.

The HOT body exposes a bounded derived presentation tier from the exact current
condition and its revision. Pose, gait, work cadence, rest/use of stations,
bounded particles/sound and appearance overlays are non-owning cues. A Villager
animation, scoreboard, nameplate, board or diagnostic cannot change condition,
keep a second state or prove that treatment occurred. Removing or replacing a
cue never heals or harms the resident.

Cues identify actionable meaning without publishing exact hidden numbers:
stable, strained, hungry/starving, exposed, wounded, infected/isolated and
recovering states must be distinguishable where they affect choices. Multiple
conditions compose deterministically under one versioned cue policy rather than
whichever adapter updated last. Success and recovery are visible as well as
deterioration.

## Exact HOT physical contact

One contact observation binds the exact resident/body lease and condition
revision, exact cause/source, loaded position or collision volume, game instant
and bounded physical evidence. Applicable causes include real attack/effect,
fire or dangerous block/fluid exposure, infection or decontamination surface,
food consumption, shelter/rest and treatment interaction.

Only reconciliation may turn that observation into a canonical condition
change. A nearby effect, aggregate settlement hazard or body with the same role
cannot stand in for contact with the named resident. The HOT observation is
accepted, rejected as stale/foreign, or retained locally ambiguous; it is never
followed by an additional COLD consequence for the same interval.

Ordinary player actions operate through their real world surfaces. Giving or
taking exact food composes with `MAT-006`; providing/removing treatment and
reagent composes with `MAT-003`; changing shelter, quarantine or contaminated
terrain changes exposure through the owning physical observation. A command,
fixture or UI control cannot directly set the asserted health result.

## COLD condition progress and HOT/COLD neutrality

COLD may advance nutrition, disease, exposure and recovery only from retained
canonical inputs and the family's declared bounded knowledge. It cannot inspect
unloaded Minecraft, infer exact contact through unknown shielding or continue
an exposure contradicted by authoritative current HOT evidence. Unknown
significant shelter, treatment or hazard facts use the declared approximation,
inspection or visible wait/failure policy.

HOT acquisition retains the exact condition revision and suspends only the
competing interval writer. Physical observations reconcile immediately; demand
loss checkpoints the current condition and unfinished care/exposure cause before
COLD resumes. Repeated switching cannot reset hunger, clear infection, repeat a
meal/treatment, avoid damage ticks or confer a systematic recovery advantage.

## Care, incapacity, death and recovery

Condition thresholds produce owned consequences for the same resident:
assignment degradation or release, rest/shelter need, exact provisioning or
treatment task, quarantine, incapacity, recovery, displacement or death. These
are typed transitions through their existing owners, not presentation policy.
A resident cannot continue remote work while physically incapacitated or under
an exclusive treatment/care claim.

Only confirmed death of the exact body/condition transition removes a living
resident and produces its owned aftermath. Missing body, unloaded chunk or
severe-looking cue never implies death. Recovery consumes the exact required
care/resources and advances meaningful stages; merely waiting near a medic or
loading a healthy model is not treatment.

Snapshot/WAL recovery retains condition revision, active causes, exact care or
provision links, confirmed contacts and ambiguous physical boundaries. A
physically started non-replayable treatment/contact becomes locally
`UNKNOWN_AFTER_RESTART` until the exact body/postcondition is naturally
inspected. Recovery cannot duplicate damage, food, medicine, death or cure, and
unrelated residents continue. Bounded history preserves causal summaries after
every active/unknown claim closes.

Condition presentation composes with the shared actor carrier. A condition may
progress COLD while the actor is inactive, but natural return must present the
current condition on the same canonical resident and stable Minecraft UUID
under one newer physical epoch. A stale/missing carrier cannot authorize a
replacement body or an obsolete healthy cue. HOT contact and COLD exposure
must fence the same interval so rapid switching cannot apply hunger, infection,
injury, treatment or recovery twice.

## MAT-010 evidence boundary

Closure requires:

- pure and codec evidence for exact condition ownership, deterministic cue
  composition and precedence, thresholds, COLD progression, duplicate/stale
  contact rejection, switching and recovery;
- focused production-owner evidence for ordinary food, exposure/infection,
  injury and treatment/recovery paths plus wrong resident/body, removed source,
  competing assignment, demand loss and graceful/abrupt restart;
- the smallest candidate-bound ordinary-player native lifecycle set proving
  exact physical contact and visible deterioration/recovery on the same named
  residents, with terminal semantic outcomes and bounded PMV3 correlation. One
  resident must worsen while unobserved, return with current cues, receive an
  exact treatment/meal and resume eligible work without replacement;
- player-height in-progress frames for the distinct applicable condition cues,
  accepted only as M2 presence evidence; and
- one coherent final critical integration gate after changed faithful lanes
  pass locally.

Existing exact ActorCondition, MAT-003 service, MAT-006 provisioning/birth,
combat/contact, quarantine and recovery evidence may compose unchanged. A
fixture may establish an initial condition or hazard but cannot inject the
asserted contact, meal, treatment, deterioration, death or recovery. A board,
diagnostic value or final health number alone is not M2. No standalone native
matrix, timing proof or repeated confidence run is required.

This target closes `MAT-010` at M2 only. M3 remains a separate unbriefed-player
gate: players must notice the meaningful condition, associate it with the same
person and local cause, choose a plausible response and recognize its result
without operator diagnostics or opaque IDs. Exact medical diagnosis may still
require an explicit local inspection surface. Final art/audio, natural-terrain
polish, balance, deployment, production cutover and v2 removal remain separate.
