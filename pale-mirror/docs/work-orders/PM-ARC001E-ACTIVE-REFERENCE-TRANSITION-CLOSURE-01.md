## Outcome

Before any new MAT breadth, every currently active producer that consumes,
transfers, replaces, retires or releases a canonical reference or physical
replica boundary uses one closed family-neutral typed transition contract. A
domain family supplies the complete subject, owner, expected revision/epoch and
one exhaustive legal disposition. One common canonical transaction atomically
closes the prior binding and establishes exactly one live successor, explicit
terminal disposition, bounded ambiguity or owner-local block.

Process-specific executors retain their domain policy and registered physical
effects. They do not implement private reference lifecycles, independently
delete old bindings, synthesize successors, publish partial custody/replica
state or rely on a later audit to complete normal work. The production boundary
must make an omitted or inapplicable partial result unconstructable or reject it
before canonical publication.

## Scope and sequencing

The active F0.6R3 order revision 19 creates this reusable boundary and migrates
the exact r13 failing chain. ARC-001E starts only after F0.6R3 product acceptance
and inventories every other active mutating producer. Previously classified
`OWNER_EXPLICIT_UNCHANGED` is accepted only when the path is read-only in the
current runtime or explicitly future and unreachable. An active mutator either
proves it already consumes the common contract or migrates.

This is a completion of the accepted ARC/REL architecture, not another relation
store, ORM, database, compatibility bridge or gameplay expansion. Fresh-world
format policy applies. MAT-006 and later breadth remain blocked until acceptance.

## Acceptance

- One inventory-complete composition guard enumerates every active transition
  producer and mechanically rejects an unregistered/private mutation path.
- The common boundary cannot publish without one complete typed disposition and
  atomically accounts for the old binding, successor/disposition and affected
  obligations.
- Family-specific policy cannot bypass the common transition or infer missing
  owner/type/epoch/result dimensions.
- Ordinary, missing/stale/duplicate, bounded-ambiguity and restart/recovery
  evidence covers the smallest representatives of each materially different
  transition shape.
- A lawful producer failure is owner-local and explainable; only already
  accepted canonical or persisted corruption may quarantine the whole runtime.
- Existing closure and recovery audits independently reject forged incomplete
  state. They are defense in depth, not the ordinary transition engine.
- Verification is dependency-aware and representative. No blanket all-family
  native matrix or unrelated product re-proof is required.
