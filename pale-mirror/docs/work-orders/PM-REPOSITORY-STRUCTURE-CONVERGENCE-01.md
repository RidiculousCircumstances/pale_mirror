decision, tactical and navigation breadth. A new contributor must be able to
identify the pack/deployment surface, active Pale Mirror modules, tests and
harnesses, engineering utilities, research experiments, generated/disposable

names or searching a mixed root.

pack/source ownership boundary, Git history, module/API ownership and Frontier
semantics. The final tree has one documented purpose and owner for every

visibly separated; equivalent tools or evidence roots do not compete; generated
and task-private outputs do not accumulate in product-source locations.

  links, ignore rules and path-validation fixtures updated together;

  classes of root/layout clutter actually removed;
