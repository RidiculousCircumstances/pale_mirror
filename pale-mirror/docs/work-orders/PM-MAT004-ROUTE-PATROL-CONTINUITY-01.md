## Product outcome and acceptance

Resume the approved materialization sequence after the accepted F0 foundation.
An assigned settlement patrol becomes one exact class-D operation that a player
can encounter mid-journey: the same named people assemble, keep a distinct
formation, traverse the retained route in COLD or HOT, inspect the exact causal
obstruction and finish, block or fail without turning into generic ambient guard
motion.

Close `MAT-004` and the route-patrol half of `V3-AUD-038` under the accepted
[route-patrol contract](../frontier-v3-route-patrol-contract.md):

- one persisted patrol owns its exact strategic task, settlement, bounded
  `RouteUnitManifest`, ordered roster, individual ingress state, distinct
  formation cells, immutable pedestrian topology, route direction/cursor,
  command state and obstruction evidence;
- admission fails visibly when exact members cannot occupy legal distinct
  ingress/formation positions. It never snaps a resident to a waypoint,
  substitutes another guard, assumes constant Y or uses a settlement anchor;
- COLD advances only retained OPEN edges and atomically translates the complete
  formation plus canonical actor locations. A HOT `ROUTE_PATROL` lease contains
  those same identities and advances the same next edge only from normal
  observed Minecraft arrival through the shared movement provider;
- generic `GUARD`, `WORK`, `PATROL` presentation, nearby bodies and Foundry
  diagnostics cannot execute or confirm the operation. Foundry remains
  read-only and may not load, clear, repair or replan a route;
- ordinary member death, changed/missing owned body, occupied next cell,
  damaged support or player/world obstruction becomes typed evidence for the
  same patrol and has a bounded owned drain/block/failure path;
- demand loss returns the unchanged formation/cursor to COLD. Graceful/recovery
  boundaries retain exact roster and progress or expose bounded
  `UNKNOWN_AFTER_RESTART`; no edge, body, death or obstruction is replayed;
- this is a fresh-world format replacement. Obsolete patrol snapshot/WAL bytes
  fail closed rather than being inferred or adapted.
