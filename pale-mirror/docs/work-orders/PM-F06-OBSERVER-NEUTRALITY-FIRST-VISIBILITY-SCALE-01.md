## Product outcome and acceptance

Close the seamless-world foundation before adding more gameplay breadth. A
neutral player watching, leaving, returning or repeatedly crossing HOT/COLD
boundaries must not start work, change its rules, reroll opportunities or grant
systematic advantage. On first arrival the player sees the current world rather
than static objects appearing in executor batches. The complete twelve-
settlement/two-seed world and concurrent hive fronts retain every exact actor
while operating within bounded server, persistence and physical-body budgets.

Complete F0.6 from the
[seamless foundation](../frontier-v3-seamless-foundation.md), FND-08/FND-09 and
the [execution semantics](../frontier-v3-execution-semantics.md):

  contract. Actor/object identities, claims, quantities, custody, completed
  stages, retained work, legal topology, confirmed effects and recovery
  discriminators remain exact in every run; statistical tolerance never masks
  one broken invariant;
- quiet production uses the same labor norms, inputs and outputs. Any allowed
  completion-time difference is attributable to a declared bounded physical or
  scheduling cause. Combat shares legal weapons, defenses, tactical constraints
  and known terrain meaning while success, casualty and duration distributions
  satisfy a predeclared fixed-seed sampling rule and versioned tolerances rather
  than identical hit sequences;
- the sample set, metrics, tolerances and decision rule are fixed before their
  measurement and retained with red results. They cannot be widened or tuned to
  make the candidate green. Actual player obstruction, damage, transfer or other
  physical intervention is a new typed input and is evaluated separately from
  neutral observation;

  wounds, targets, commitments and process/actor/event-keyed random
  opportunities. Neutral switching gives no systematic benefit or penalty, and
  an actual player change survives leave/return and graceful/abrupt recovery on
  the exact target without reset or replay;

  player admission. Dynamic canonical state catches up to its current revision
  before a managed object becomes interactable or scene-eligible. A previously
  observed chunk reconciles its replica/aftermath without overwriting unknown
  drift. No settlement, route or hive object may visibly pop into an already
  exposed chunk through a later executor batch;

  authorities and its distributed/two-seed hive. Concurrent bounded fronts use
  the same exact actors, bodies, leases, cargo and outcomes as smaller flows;
  scale cannot aggregate people/bioforms, introduce a second combat state,
  silently fall back to COLD or grow an unbounded queue;
- read-only diagnostics expose enough correlated state to judge the process
  cursor, front, lease epoch, replica revision, deferred aftermath and failure
  class without mutating canonical/world state or force-loading chunks.
