The observable contract is:

1. One shared registered adapter derives a deterministic replica fingerprint
   and provenance for an exact owned settlement depot or hive store. Pure code
   retains identities/revisions/policy; Minecraft code only reads or changes a
   naturally loaded exact block entity and submits typed facts. No chunk is
   force-loaded and no player is required for physical eligibility.
2. Initial projection and later catch-up cross explicit durable boundaries.
   Before any write to an existing owned chest, the adapter compares its exact
   identity, provenance and contents with the current retained replica/epoch.
   An unchanged replica may be updated idempotently to later canonical state;
   missing, foreign or changed evidence is durably classified before any write.
3. Custody is exclusive, smallest-container scoped and temporary. Acquire uses
   the latest matching evidence; checkpoint/release use exact canonical,
   evidence and epoch fences. A naturally loaded zero-player scope may hold
   custody. A safely unloaded serialized scope does not. Unresolved recovery
   blocks only its exact container and cannot fabricate release or reuse epoch.
4. Settlement production and hive growth make eligibility decisions from
   canonical inputs plus current custody, never from historical
   `ContainerSurfaceStatus.ACTIVE`, presentation demand or previous visit.
   With no live/unresolved custodian, COLD may advance. With a live custodian,
   only the fenced physical path may change that exact container. Other
   settlements/nests/processes remain usable.
5. Never-visited, visited-then-safely-unloaded and naturally loaded-with-zero-
   players histories have equivalent work admission, due ordering, exact input
   consumption/output, reservations and terminal canonical state for each
   reference family. A visit or presentation-mode switch creates no work,
   quantity, duplicate due edge or systematic advantage.
6. Returning to an unchanged serialized chest catches it up exactly once from
   current canonical state. An ordinary chest-content change becomes retained
   actual fingerprint/provenance conflict before projection; the player/world
   content is not overwritten, credited as capacity or silently adopted.
7. Graceful and abrupt restart preserve the last confirmed replica/custody
   boundary. Restart around acquire/checkpoint/release or a changed observation
   cannot double-consume biomass/wheat, duplicate bread/growth, lose an epoch or
   globally stall unrelated COLD work.
8. Legacy surface state may remain temporarily for unconverted families and
   presentation geometry, but it is non-authoritative for both reference flows.
   Their exact production `ACTIVE` branches must disappear and the checked debt
   baseline must strictly fall without a renamed realization boolean/helper.
   Diagnostics expose canonical stock, replica revision/conflict and current
   custody separately.

## Knowledge and fail-closed policy

For both families, actual chest contents/provenance are `KNOWN` only from the
currently naturally loaded exact block entity and its retained observation.
Serialized replica bytes are evidence of the last confirmed projection, not
proof of current physical custody. Unloaded geometry/content is `UNKNOWN` for a
new physical action but does not prevent canonical COLD work when no live or
unresolved custody exists. Unknown capacity cannot authorize an irreversible
dependent action. A changed/foreign/missing loaded chest produces a local typed
conflict; it never causes a force-load, invented observation, automatic repair,
whole-world wait or fallback to `ACTIVE`.
