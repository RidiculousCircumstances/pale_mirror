## Product outcome and acceptance

A running zero-player world continues settlement work before any settlement
chunk is visited. The first neutral arrival only changes the execution provider:
it materializes the exact current canonical harvest worker, job, stage, cursor
and already-due physical aftermath. It neither creates, starts nor resets that
work to the first field cell.

The same first arrival presents complete current hive geometry, not labels over
empty terrain with only cocoon bodies visible. While HOT, every managed
resident and bioform remains an ordinary colliding, gravity-bound Minecraft
entity. Canonical position is process truth and a movement target; it is not a
permission to suspend a body in air after its physical support is removed.

The deployed F0.6R1 world contradicts this promise. After about fifteen hours
of server uptime, first natural visits to settlements 2, 3, 4, 8 and 12 each
admitted their exact field-1 harvest lease at `crop-0`. The same jobs advanced
only while subsequently HOT (including later `crop-2`, `crop-7` and `crop-10`
leases). This repeated cross-settlement pattern is accepted live defect
evidence, not a request for another discovery campaign.

The same user session found that a first-visited hive displayed floating organ
labels and cocoons but not its actual organ structures. Subsequent ordinary
block breaks were attributed by the runtime to exact west-hive organs, proving
that canonical/provenance ownership exists while the visible geometry is
missing or incoherent. The user also found multiple managed mobs suspended one
block above the surface; removing blocks under them leaves them at their prior
Y level instead of invoking ordinary Minecraft gravity. These are accepted
product observations. Reproducing their existence broadly is not the task.

Acceptance requires:

- a fresh or identity-bound world runs through a deterministic interval with
  zero players and without loading or demanding the selected settlement;
- during that interval, an eligible exact harvest process starts and makes
  meaningful COLD progress under the ordinary production rules. Its worker,
  job, stage, cursor, labor/accounting facts and deferred crop/output aftermath
  remain durable and inspectable;
- a first ordinary natural visit to at least two independently selected
  settlements admits the state current at that instant. It must not reset or
  reconstruct either job at `crop-0`, and player demand must not be the causal
  predecessor of its creation or prior progress;
- first visibility reconciles current crop cells and other due aftermath before
  the managed field becomes scene-eligible or interactable. It does not
  force-load an unvisited field, silently mutate unloaded Minecraft blocks,
  replay a cinematic history or mint output outside its existing effect owner;
- at first natural visibility, both declared seed-nest scopes have their exact
  current organ tissue, foundations, infection surface, cocoons and labels in
  one coherent presentation. A label may not advertise a usable organ over
  missing geometry; incomplete/foreign geometry stays explicitly blocked and
  non-interactable rather than being silently accepted;
- a bounded representative resident and bioform test proves that loaded managed
  bodies use ordinary Minecraft gravity and collision both while moving and
  while idle. Removing their physical support is a legitimate player input:
  they fall/land through normal physics and the owning canonical boundary
  observes or types the resulting displacement/damage. They do not hover,
  teleport back each tick, ignore collision or acquire an ambient second route;
- the same exact farmer/job/cursor then continues HOT, releases on ordinary
  departure and resumes COLD without duplicated work, skipped accounting or a
  second progress clock;
- obstruction, player intervention, worker loss and recovery remain typed and
  scoped. All unaffected F0.1--F0.6, F0.6R1 and MAT evidence is reused;
- verification is proportional: focused deterministic/provider regression,
  the smallest faithful real-server first-visit/physics carrier and one final
  coherent critical/package gate. No broad terrain, calibration, pressure/JFR or
  infrastructure re-proof is part of this correction;
- after PM accepts the candidate, deploy its exact JAR to a preserved or new
  disposable live world as format safety requires, verify ordinary startup and
  leave it available for the user's direct first-visit retest. Automation does
  not claim human/M3 acceptance.
