## Required result

- One family-neutral versioned progress-obligation contract is supplied by the
  registered authoritative process/execution descriptor and evaluated without
  concrete-family branches or production-reducer-as-oracle reuse.
- The verifier uses canonical `SimInstant` for domain liveness and a separate
  wall-clock health boundary for JVM/client/harness failures.
- Dynamic relation-based subject discovery binds the current process, worker,
  claim/lease, intent and successor; ID prefixes, fixed epochs and sole-match
  inference are forbidden.
- The existing OBS envelope is transported through one bounded session-scoped
  local stream or equivalent incremental view with sequence/gap/completeness
  semantics. It never blocks the mutation lane, force-loads chunks or becomes
  another state authority.
- Its ordinary developer-facing consumer follows the dynamically discovered
  current subject and emits change-only semantic milestones, obligation state,
  exact next expectation, owner bindings and the first missing/divergent edge;
  it does not require Terra to poll or grep raw server text. The first violation
  atomically publishes one latest-incident pointer and deterministic actionable
  synopsis, while reconnect loss is an explicit evidence gap rather than a
  fresh apparently healthy session.
- Verdicts distinguish `SATISFIED`, `VIOLATED`, live `PENDING` and terminal
  `INCONCLUSIVE`. Only complete `SATISFIED` obligations can promote a candidate.
- First violation produces one identity-complete, deduplicated and bounded
  incident capsule with deterministic synopsis, causal tail, exact subject
  view, expected/observed semantic fingerprints, operator result, linked frame,
  replay recipe and checksums. Missing evidence remains visible.
- Equivalent capsules group under one stable defect identity with explicit
  owner, affected product promise, first/last occurrence, cumulative age/cost,
  observed-versus-inferred cause, fix identity, regression identity and
  `OBSERVED`/`TRIAGED`/`FIX_CANDIDATE`/`VERIFIED`/`RECURRENT` lifecycle. A new
  revision or world cannot erase or silently close the defect.
- Eligible capsules replay canonical decisions without repeating irreversible
  Minecraft effects and identify the first divergent semantic boundary and
  component. Fixed capsules enter a bounded regression corpus.
- The graphical promotion receipt is executable: it cannot pass the harvest
  vertical without first visibility, maturity, admission, continuous worker/
  cell progress, terminal harvest, custody/production and successor facts.
- The graphical pilot proves observer noninterference for every evidence-bearing
  interval. Exact player body/clearance, demand and camera relocation are
  declared inputs; an overlap with a route/station/effect envelope yields
  explicit `HARNESS_INTERFERENCE`/`INCONCLUSIVE`, never a product failure or a
  silent retry.
- Optional OTel/JFR projection is off-thread, bounded and non-authoritative.
  Exact IDs never become metric labels; exporter failure, drops and overflow
  are visible and cannot change simulation output.

## Acceptance stories

1. A deterministic field advances to 6/7 and then silently emits no permitted
   progress or typed blocker. The verifier fails at the declared simulation-
   tick deadline before the general wall-clock timeout and creates a complete
   linked capsule.
2. The same lifecycle reaches a legitimate typed obstruction/wait with exact
   owner and disposition. It does not produce a false unexplained-stall verdict,
   while product promotion remains truthful about the blocker.
3. A normal dynamically discovered harvest reaches its exact receipt, custody/
   production and declared successor. The full-pack graphical preflight and
   machine promotion receipt agree.
4. Restart during an open obligation either resumes the exact obligation and
   causal identity or returns explicit `INCONCLUSIVE`/typed recovery evidence;
   it can never become a green endpoint by losing history.
5. One retained incident replays with the exact compatible identities and
   points to the first divergent boundary. An incompatible build/schema or
   incomplete capsule rejects explicitly without executing physical effects.
6. Stream loss, queue pressure, capsule truncation and optional exporter outage
   are bounded and visible; telemetry enabled/disabled produces identical
   canonical hashes. Same-seed overhead stays within the declared Frontier
   tick/storage budgets.
7. Two equivalent failures in different worlds update one defect group; a
   changed surface symptom with the same first divergent boundary does not reset
   its cost. The original contradiction plus its retained regression are both
   required for `VERIFIED`, and a later recurrence moves it to `RECURRENT`.
8. One long-running developer follow session automatically binds the current
   subject, reports only semantic changes, survives a compatible restart by
   sequence continuation and, on an injected missing edge, produces the same
   incident/synopsis as the promotion gate without manual log inspection.
9. A graphical observer is deliberately placed inside the current worker route.
   The run names the exact overlap and terminates as harness interference. The
   same unchanged binary at a pre-proven non-overlapping pose can then produce
   product evidence without losing or rewriting the first occurrence.

## Scope and economy

Reuse accepted OBS-001 reason registry, envelope, incident index, traces and
bundles; the existing test pilot, frames and operator diagnostics; and current
descriptor/relationship identities. Do not introduce a database, workflow
engine, second event store, remote mandatory service or dashboard dependency.

The first reusable proof is harvest plus the smallest materially different
duration-bearing process needed to reject a crop-specific abstraction. Other
families adopt the contract when changed or when their milestone requires it.
No all-family native matrix, observability benchmark campaign or repeated
confidence run is authorized. Iterate with the cheapest focused model/negative
checks; run the applicable critical and graphical terminal gates once on the
frozen candidate.

Useful instrumentation is measured by reduced diagnosis/reproduction time and
truthful automatic rejection, not event count. The delivery reports incident-
to-explanation timing, replay eligibility/success, first-divergence result,
overhead/bounds, exact source/tree/JAR identity, remaining gaps and repository/
process state. It does not claim M3, deployment or gameplay completion.

## Exclusions

- no canonical mutation, repair, retry or work scheduling by the verifier;
- no raw-log parsing as semantic authority;
- no wall-clock domain progress assertion;
- no full-world dumps or unbounded per-tick traces;
- no external issue tracker, Grafana/Tempo/Loki/Collector cluster or hosted
  telemetry requirement in this slice;
- no push, tag, history rewrite, v2 removal, MAT breadth or unrelated cleanup.
