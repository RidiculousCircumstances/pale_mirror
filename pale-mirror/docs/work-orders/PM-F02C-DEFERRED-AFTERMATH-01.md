## Product outcome and exclusions

When a justified event happens outside loaded Minecraft space, the autonomous
world must commit the consequences it can actually know at event time. It must
not wait for a player, pretend to know unknown shielding, or replay the original
explosion/action later. Instead it retains a bounded causal footprint. On
natural later loading, current world evidence is inspected and only the still
valid physical aftermath is materialized once; conflicting player/world changes
remain intact and isolate only their affected footprint.

Use one real unrestricted explosion and one constructive obstruction as the
reference verticals. Terra owns selection of the smallest coherent existing
processes, local design, algorithms, aggregate shape, helpers, file choices and
test commands. Reuse existing effect/observation/intent owners where coherent;
do not add a parallel simulation, family-specific aftermath ledger or scene
authority.

Excluded: F0.3 resource lots and partial stacks; F0.4 navigation/tactics/fronts;
general explosion/combat breadth; natural-terrain hardening; protected zones;
blanket repair of foreign/player blocks; production deployment; v2 removal;
M3/readability; provider-pipeline repair; F0.2B physical-debt reproof.

## Observable contract

1. One pure, persisted and bounded semantic-consequence/aftermath owner retains
   cause identity, event time, observation time separately, provenance,
   declared knowledge/approximation, expected revisions/preconditions and a
   chunk-indexed resolution cursor. Registered Minecraft providers may observe
   or realize it but cannot decide the canonical consequence.
2. A COLD explosion advances without presentation demand or force-load. It
   commits only casualties/damage justified by valid retained facts. Unknown
   shielding cannot justify an exact hidden casualty. Its later physical record
   is an aftermath description, never a queued projectile or replayed explosion.
3. Natural loading first observes current evidence. Still-valid owned cells are
   changed idempotently; a known late wall, foreign block or player construction
   is not overwritten merely because it lies inside an old footprint. The
   contradiction becomes typed local evidence and the unaffected footprint may
   continue.
4. Constructive capacity is not credited to an irreversible dependent process
   until its required physical/evidence validity exists. A real obstruction or
   unknown target yields a bounded inspect, replan or visible abandonment result
   for the exact process; it neither invents capacity nor stalls unrelated work.
5. HOT and COLD routes share the same cause and canonical consequence rules.
   HOT may use an observed physical result; COLD retains later aftermath. A
   scene boundary, player arrival or mode switch cannot duplicate, erase,
   broaden or backdate the event.
6. Recovery distinguishes prepared, canonically committed, partially realized,
   confirmed and locally ambiguous boundaries. Restart at any applicable point
   neither replays the explosion, duplicates damage, loses already confirmed
   cells nor treats an implementation-owned write as foreign drift.
7. Retention is explicitly bounded by causes, chunks and cells/work units.
   Duplicate/stale cause or epoch, oversized footprint, wrong provenance,
   contradictory precondition and out-of-order receipt fail closed. Resolved
   units compact only after their terminal evidence is durable; one conflicted
   unit cannot cause a global frontier wait.
