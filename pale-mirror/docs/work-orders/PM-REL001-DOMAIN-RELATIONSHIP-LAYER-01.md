## Acceptance boundary

- One closed contract covers stable relationship kinds, typed endpoint kinds,
  one canonical owner, cardinality/exclusivity, lifecycle compatibility,
  retention and failure disposition. Unknown/duplicate/incompatible declarations
  fail closed.
- Authoritative relationships remain ordinary canonical aggregate/event facts.
  The incoming/outgoing relationship view is deterministic, bounded, read-only
  and reconstructible; removing it cannot change simulation behavior.
- The complete active F0.6R3 vertical is migrated without dual authority. From
  any named task, order, job, worker, input/output, allocation, recipient,
  lease/carrier or successor, bounded inspection reaches the same exact chain,
  current owner/revision and first blocking relationship if one exists.
- Two same-kind blocked bread tasks, another eligible worker, another bread
  item and missing/duplicate/stale/wrong-type endpoint cases cannot cause
  re-selection. They produce the contract's typed smallest-owner outcome and
  preserve unrelated progress.
- Snapshot plus ordered WAL recovery restores the same authoritative relations
  and relationship view before due actions or physical execution resume. No
  relation depends on JVM object identity, map iteration order or wall time.
- The actor live-binding/inactive-carrier relation stays evidence-only and does
  not acquire actor, assignment, route, inventory or simulation authority.
- A closed current-family inventory classifies every long-lived cross-owner
  relation under the contract. The current vertical is fully current; untouched
  families have an explicit no-new-breadth migration boundary rather than a
  parallel generic graph or speculative mass rewrite.
- Architecture/static checks reject a newly introduced unregistered durable
  cross-owner link or post-admission attribute inference where mechanically
  detectable. Representative negative and restart coverage protects the
  semantic rule where static proof is insufficient.
- Existing exact identity, conservation, HOT/COLD ownership, conflict incident,
  bounded work and player-causality guarantees are preserved.

## Exclusions

- No SQLite/JDBC, ORM, generic CRUD repository, lazy loading, graph database or
  second event/state store.
- No adapter/Minecraft tag or diagnostic index may become relationship authority.
- No broad gameplay, natural-terrain, balance, MAT-006 birth, remote push,
  production deployment, v2 removal or confidence-only native matrix.
- Do not redesign unrelated aggregates merely to make every scalar field look
  like an edge. The contract covers semantically durable cross-owner identity.
- Do not weaken exact endpoints into string conventions or retain a permanent
  legacy inference path beside the migrated F0.6R3 vertical.
