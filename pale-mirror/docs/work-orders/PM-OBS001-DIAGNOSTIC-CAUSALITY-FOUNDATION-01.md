## Product outcome and acceptance

An operator starting from a stalled or conflicted Frontier object can obtain
one bounded, restart-stable explanation that connects its canonical owner and
state to the responsible command/schedule, HOT or COLD execution, lease,
physical intent/observation, reconciliation result, exact reason and recovery
disposition. The same typed facts drive machine manifests and human output, so
a green summary cannot contradict an underlying required conflict or error.

Complete the foundation in
[`frontier-v3-diagnostic-plane.md`](../frontier-v3-diagnostic-plane.md): closed
outcome/reason semantics, the common diagnostic envelope and causal timeline,
bounded `inspect why/trace/incident`, automatic bounded incident capture and
truthful aggregate verdicts. Every current terminal conflict, recovery unknown,
invariant quarantine and adapter/infrastructure failure producer is covered by
the registry contract. Expected-but-absent selection, assignment, actor
adoption and physical materialization also require an owned typed reason rather
than diagnostic silence. Representative full-chain and ordinary-player paths
prove composition without rerunning every accepted gameplay family.
Reuse the accepted F0.6R3 trace vertical; this order owns the remaining producer
inventory, general contracts, incident/query coverage and representative
cross-family composition.

The global strict no-inference boundary applies to diagnostics too. Each
authoritative producer stamps the complete nominal reason, category, owner,
typed subject and disposition at the first admissible failure/non-progress
boundary. The registry validates that exact declared tuple and its required
evidence; it may not choose a reason, category, owner or subject kind from an
exception class/message, generic `CONFLICT` state, identifier spelling,
collection membership, payload shape, sole compatible candidate or fallback.
An incomplete or legacy diagnostic fact is explicitly unsupported/incomplete
and fails closed before it can drive a verdict or recovery decision. Renderers
may derive text only after validation and can never become classification
authority. Focused recurrence coverage must remove or forge every tuple
dimension independently and prove that no aggregate can become green.

The slice remains diagnostic infrastructure. It may not change domain outcomes,
HOT/COLD cadence, recovery guarantees, materialization meaning or the player
promise to simplify a trace. It does not install a remote observability stack,
make JFR/OpenTelemetry authoritative, or claim M2/M3 gameplay acceptance.
